Chapter I · Wilmington Weddings

River, oaks, and open ocean.

Wilmington pairs a historic riverfront downtown with live oaks draped in Spanish moss and some of the prettiest beaches on the Carolina coast. Couples can marry in a garden, celebrate downtown, and take portraits on the sand, all in one day.

I photograph and film Wilmington weddings with an editorial eye and a documentary heart, so your gallery holds both the beauty of the place and the honest moments between the people in it.

Chapter II · Good to Know

Planning a Wilmington wedding.

A few things I talk through with Wilmington couples. I also photograph weddings in Outer Banks, Virginia Beach & Hampton Roads, Williamsburg, Richmond, Raleigh-Durham, and anywhere your day takes you.

  1. 01

    Garden ceremonies

    Wilmington's gardens and historic homes are popular for ceremonies and portraits, and many book up early in spring when the azaleas bloom.

  2. 02

    Beach portraits

    Wrightsville, Carolina, and Kure beaches are all close to town. Each beach town has its own rules for ceremonies, so check before you plan one on the sand.

  3. 03

    Riverfront evenings

    Downtown along the Cape Fear River is lovely after dark. A few evening portraits with the lights on the water make a great close to the day.

  4. 04

    Hurricane season

    Late summer and early fall carry some storm risk on the coast. I always talk through a backup plan for outdoor ceremonies.
