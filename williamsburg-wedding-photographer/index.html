Chapter I · Williamsburg Weddings

History with a heartbeat.

Williamsburg weddings carry a sense of place you can feel in every frame: brick paths and colonial architecture, old estates along the James River, and vineyards that glow at the end of the day.

I photograph and film Williamsburg weddings with a timeless, editorial approach that suits the setting, while keeping the documentary moments that make the day yours: the laughter, the nerves, the hands held a little tighter.

Chapter II · Good to Know

Planning a Williamsburg wedding.

A few things I talk through with Williamsburg couples. I also photograph weddings in Outer Banks, Virginia Beach & Hampton Roads, Richmond, Raleigh-Durham, Wilmington, and anywhere your day takes you.

  1. 01

    Historic-area portraits

    Colonial Williamsburg and the surrounding historic sites make beautiful backdrops, and some have their own photography rules. Check with your venue or the site ahead of time.

  2. 02

    Fall is the season

    October and early November bring warm color and mild evenings, and dates book early. Spring is a close second.

  3. 03

    Vineyard and estate light

    Open lawns and long views mean golden hour does a lot of the work. I plan portrait time around sunset for your date.

  4. 04

    Weekend celebrations

    Many Williamsburg weddings are destination weekends. I can cover welcome dinners and farewell brunches as well as the wedding day.
