Chapter I · Raleigh-Durham Weddings

The Triangle, told well.

Raleigh, Durham, and Chapel Hill offer some of the most varied wedding settings in North Carolina: university chapels and gardens, restored tobacco warehouses, modern downtown spaces, and rolling farm venues just outside the city.

I photograph and film weddings across the Triangle with an editorial, documentary approach: honest moments, natural color, and portraits that feel like you.

Chapter II · Good to Know

Planning a Triangle wedding.

A few things I talk through with Raleigh-Durham couples. I also photograph weddings in Outer Banks, Virginia Beach & Hampton Roads, Williamsburg, Richmond, Wilmington, and anywhere your day takes you.

  1. 01

    Gardens and campuses

    The Triangle's gardens and campus landmarks are favorite portrait spots, and several require reservations or permits for wedding photography. Book them with your venue early.

  2. 02

    Farm and barn venues

    Open fields and tree lines give soft, even light. I plan portraits for the last hour before sunset.

  3. 03

    Warehouse and downtown venues

    Brick, steel, and big windows suit an editorial look. I bring lighting for receptions so evening coverage stays clean and true to color.

  4. 04

    Spring and fall

    April, May, October, and November are the busiest months in the Triangle. Pollen season in early spring is worth knowing about for outdoor ceremonies.
