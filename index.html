Chapter V · The Author

Hi, I’m Aigbokhai.

I’ve always wanted a way to capture the world as it changes. It started with a Canon AE-1 Program and photos of my friends and family, documenting the real love shared between people. That is still what I’m chasing.

I love people and make friends easily, so when a friend asked me to second shoot his wedding, it felt completely natural. I was already documenting real relationships; now I got to document people on the happiest day of their lives. More than eleven years later, I still can’t believe I get to do this, and I feel incredibly lucky.

Every wedding day is special. On the day I’m calm, joyful and organized. I pay attention to the setting and bring whichever of my skills the day needs. Couples almost always mention how calm I am — that’s simply who I am.

I also shoot real film and develop it in my own lab. Its character can’t be replicated, it tells a story beautifully, and the slower process has made me a better artist.

Off the clock, you’ll find me in the yard working on one of a thousand projects. The truck or the yard? Depends on my mood.

— Aigbokhai

Appendix · Good to know

Questions couples ask.

How far in advance should we book you for our wedding?

Most couples book 9 to 12 months ahead, and spring and fall Saturdays go first. Shorter notice is welcome too, so if your date is coming up soon, send an inquiry and I will tell you right away if I am available. I reply to every inquiry within 24 hours.

Do you also offer video?

Yes. Photo and film come from one team, so your gallery and your film share one plan, one timeline and one look. The Photo + Video Collection includes a 3 to 5 minute cinematic highlight film, elopements can add a short film, and I also shoot Super 8 and 16mm motion film for a timeless keepsake reel.

Do you shoot film?

Yes. Alongside digital coverage I shoot 35mm and medium format film and develop it in my own lab, and I offer Super 8 and 16mm motion film. Film can be added to any collection for the grain, color and timeless feel that only real film gives.

How long will it take to see our pictures?

Your fully edited gallery and film are delivered within four weeks, and often sooner. Every image is individually edited and color-graded, and you receive an online gallery to share with family plus a print release.

How many pictures will we get?

There is no fixed number, because every wedding is different. A long day with a big family and a full reception naturally produces more than a small elopement. You receive a fully edited gallery with every moment worth keeping, and nothing held back to sell later.

Do you work with assistants or other photographers?

I lead and photograph every wedding myself. For bigger days, the Two-Photographer and Photo + Video collections add a second shooter or a filmmaker, so both sides of the getting-ready, the ceremony and the dance floor are covered.

What's your backup plan if you can't photograph our wedding?

In 11 years, I have never missed a wedding. Still, you are covered: I have a team of photographers I have worked with for over 10 years, plus a wide network of trusted professionals ready to step in if there is ever an emergency, so your day is photographed no matter what.

Do you have insurance?

Yes, Aikhoje Studios is fully insured. If your venue needs a certificate of insurance, just let me know and I will send it over.

Still wondering about something? Ask me directly — I reply to every inquiry within 24 hours.
