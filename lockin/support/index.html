Common questions

My heart rate or history isn't showing.

Lock In needs permission to use Apple Health. On your iPhone, open Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Lock In and turn on the data types.

Distance, routes, or the soccer heatmap are missing.

These use your Apple Watch's location. On your iPhone, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Lock In and choose While Using the App. Workouts still run without location; you just won't get distance or maps.

How do saved soccer pitches work?

Measure a pitch once by standing at its corner flags. It's saved and suggested automatically when you're nearby. You can always measure a new pitch, even if one is saved nearby. Manage pitches in the iPhone app under Settings → Saved Pitches.

I got a new Apple Watch. Are my pitches gone?

No. Your iPhone keeps a copy and sends them to the new watch when you open Lock In on it.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Workouts run on iPhone on their own. Heart rate, GPS distance, jump counting, and soccer tracking need an Apple Watch.

Is the jump count exact?

It's estimated from your watch's motion sensor and is usually close. Very low, fast hops can be under-counted.

Health note

Lock In offers general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Stop if you feel sharp or worsening pain, and check with a professional if you have an injury.
