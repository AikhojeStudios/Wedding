Lock In

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Lock In doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no Lock In servers. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What's stored, and where

  • Workouts you finish, with heart rate, distance, steps, and routes, are saved to Apple Health on your devices. You control this data in the Health app. Lock In reads Health data only to show live stats and your workout history.
  • Settings, such as voice cues, units, and heart rate, are stored in the app on your iPhone.
  • Saved soccer pitches are stored only on your Apple Watch and iPhone and synced directly between them, so your iPhone keeps a backup for a new or reset watch. They're never uploaded.
  • Location is used on your Apple Watch during runs, walks, and soccer matches to measure distance and speed, map your route, and build your soccer heatmap. It's saved only as part of the workout's route in Apple Health.
  • Route maps in History are shown with Apple Maps, which loads map tiles from Apple. Lock In doesn't send your data to show them.
  • CSV export happens only when you tap Export CSV, through the share sheet, to wherever you choose.

Deleting your data

Deleting Lock In deletes its settings and saved pitches from that device. Your workouts stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.

Children

Lock In doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
