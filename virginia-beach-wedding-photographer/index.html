Virginia Beach & Hampton Roads Weddings

Ocean, bay,
and city.

Hampton Roads gives couples every kind of backdrop within a short drive: the Virginia Beach oceanfront, quieter sand at Sandbridge, Chesapeake Bay beaches where the sun sets over the water, and the historic streets and gardens of Norfolk.

I photograph and film weddings across Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Suffolk, and Hampton with an editorial eye and a documentary heart. Many couples here are military families, and I plan around tight schedules, deployments, and dress uniforms with care.

Good to Know

Planning a Hampton Roads wedding.

A few things I talk through with couples marrying in Virginia Beach and the surrounding cities. I also photograph weddings in Outer Banks, Williamsburg, Richmond, Raleigh-Durham, Wilmington, and anywhere your day takes you. See the full portfolio.

  • Bay-side sunsets

    The oceanfront faces east, but Chesapeake Bay beaches face north and west, so sunset portraits there can glow over the water.

  • Military weddings

    Saber arches, dress uniforms, and families flying in on short notice. I keep the timeline flexible and make sure the traditions are covered.

  • City and coast in one day

    Getting ready downtown and portraits on the sand are an easy pairing here. I plan travel time between locations into your timeline.

  • Peak season

    Summer weekends at the oceanfront are busy. Early-morning or weekday sessions give you open beaches and soft light.
