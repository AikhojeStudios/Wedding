Chapter I · Richmond Weddings

Classic, creative, and all your own.

Richmond weddings run from black-tie ballrooms to industrial warehouse parties, garden ceremonies, and riverside celebrations. It is a city with real character, and I love a wedding that leans into it.

I photograph and film Richmond weddings with an editorial eye for the architecture and a documentary heart for the people, so the gallery feels like your day in your city, not a template.

Chapter II · Good to Know

Planning a Richmond wedding.

A few things I talk through with Richmond couples. I also photograph weddings in Outer Banks, Virginia Beach & Hampton Roads, Williamsburg, Raleigh-Durham, Wilmington, and anywhere your day takes you.

  1. 01

    Gardens and grounds

    Richmond's public gardens and historic estates are popular for ceremonies and portraits, and many require a permit or booking for photography. Ask early.

  2. 02

    City portraits

    Brick streets, murals, and old warehouses make strong editorial backdrops. A short portrait walk between ceremony and reception goes a long way.

  3. 03

    Summer heat

    July and August can be hot and humid. I plan shaded portrait spots and lean on evening light so everyone stays comfortable.

  4. 04

    Every tradition

    Richmond weddings often blend cultures and traditions. I have photographed ceremonies of many faiths and backgrounds and plan coverage around each one.
